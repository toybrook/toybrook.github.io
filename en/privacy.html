Privacy policy

This is a translation for your convenience. If it differs from the Korean original, the Korean original applies.

Toybrook only collects what it needs to send you news emails. The "Send feedback" form collects no personal information. This site and the game send grouped usage stats with no cookies (section 10). You can turn them off in the game's settings.

1. What we collect

Just one email address.

2. What it's used for

Only to email you about Toybrook's open beta and updates. We don't use it for anything else or pass it to anyone else.

You don't have to agree. If you don't, you just won't get the news emails.

3. How long we keep it

We delete it 1 year after the last news email. If you ask us to delete it before then, we delete it right away. When we delete it, we remove it from both the sign-up form and the mailing list so it can't be recovered.

4. Children under 14

Please don't sign up if you're under 14. If we learn that a sign-up is from someone under 14, we delete it right away.

5. Service providers and storage abroad

The sign-up form uses an outside service. We trust that service with receiving and storing email addresses, so your email address may be stored on that service's servers abroad.

6. Consent to news emails

Open beta and update emails count as promotional messages, so we only send them to people who separately agreed to receive them.

  • We put "(광고)" ("Ad") at the start of the subject and explain how to stop receiving them in every email.
  • Every 2 years from the day you agreed, we ask again whether you'd like to keep receiving them.
  • We don't send them between 9 p.m. and 8 a.m. (Korea time).

7. Your rights

You can ask us at any time to show, correct or delete your information, or to stop using it. Just email us at the address in section 8.

8. Withdrawing consent and deletion requests

To stop the emails or have your information deleted, email us at the address below. We delete it as soon as we see your email and reply to you.

Email: toybrook1107@gmail.com

9. How we keep it safe

Only one person, the operator, can see the sign-up list. The form account uses 2-step verification, and we don't copy the list anywhere else.

10. Usage stats (no cookies)

To improve the game and see how much it's used, this site and the game only send usage stats that are counted in groups across many people. No cookies, and no ads.

  • This site: which pages were viewed, and taps on "Play now" (which button only, once a day).
  • The game: 30 events a day at most. Opening the game (once a day, with where you came from as just one of four: Threads, Instagram, other or direct), hatching your first egg (once), coming back the day after you first started (once), the name of each habitat you enter (once a day per habitat), sending friends on a visit and welcoming guests (counts only; not which friend or to whom), your daily gift day, and errors (the first 120 characters of the error and its line in the game files, your screen width range at the time (for example 360–399), the name of the habitat you were in, and whether the graphics connection was lost once; the same error once a day).
  • To see how far our posts reach: when your time on the game screen that day passes 1, 3 and 10 minutes (once a day each, not counting time the screen was hidden, with which day it is), and on your first day, opening the nest for the first time, placing your first decor and hatching your third egg (once each).
  • To make the first few days less boring: on your first day, when you hatched your first three eggs (only which range: within 30 seconds, 1 minute, 3 minutes or later), your first pet, finishing each first-day goal (which goal only), seeing the "come back tomorrow for a Legendary egg" note, claiming a "First-week gift" (which slot only), and seeing and tapping the "Continue in your browser" bar inside the Instagram or Threads browser (only whether you chose copy or Chrome). Each once.
  • To see how accessories are used: taking a secret accessory off a friend and putting one on (which accessory only, once a day per accessory).
  • To hear how the game feels: after you've looked at the game screen for over 3 minutes that day (10 minutes on your first day), the one face you pick on the "How's Toybrook?" bar that shows up once (Love it, It's okay or Not really, once a day). If you don't pick one, nothing is sent. "Leave a note" opens the feedback form in section 12.
  • Rough ranges only, to tune the in-game values (hearts, prices, levels) for the full launch: buying decor (small, big or play item, and price range ♥0–49, 50–99 or 100+), expanding land (habitat and stage), reaching a level that's a multiple of 5 (5, 10, 15…), the range of hearts earned and spent the last day you played (0, 1–49, 50–119 or 120+, once the next time you open the game), and the number of collection entries filled (in steps of 10, once a day). All are sent with how many days it's been since you first started (0–29, or 30+), never with a date or time.
  • The game's language: each game event above is sent with which language you play in: 한국어, English or 日本語 (one of ko, en, ja).
  • Sent along with it: browser, operating system and device type, screen size, language, rough country and region, and the referring site.
  • Never sent: your name, email, contact details, device IDs, your game save as it is (which animals, exact heart count, decor layout), backup codes, or search terms in the address bar.
  • Your internet address (IP) reaches the stats provider when you connect. They use it to find a rough country and region and mix it with a value that changes every month into a visitor mark that can't be reversed, and don't keep it in the stats records. It may still remain in that provider's operations and security logs.

12. The "Send feedback" form

"Send feedback" in the game and on this site is a different form from the news email sign-up. This form collects no personal information.

  • What it asks: the habitat you mostly played in, a fun score (1–5), what you liked, what felt inconvenient or strange, and anything else you'd like to say. You don't have to answer any of them.
  • When opened from the game, the "Game info" box is filled in for you. It holds only the game version, the habitat you were in and how many animals are out.
  • "Report a problem" in the game's settings copies a short text with the game version, device type (operating system, browser, screen size), the habitat you were in and the recent errors left on this device. It's never sent automatically; you decide whether to paste it into the form. Animal names and save contents aren't included. Up to 20 recent errors are kept in this device's browser. If usage stats are on, only the start and location of each error are sent separately, as in section 10.
  • We never ask for your name, email address or contact details. You can send it without signing in to Google.
  • Please don't write personal information such as names or contact details in the text boxes. If you do, we delete it as soon as we see it.
  • Only the operator reads your feedback, and it's only used to improve the game.
  • In the "My decor code" box, please paste only a decor code copied from the game. A code holds only where decor is placed (section 13).

13. Decor codes and featured gardens

Decor codes and "Garden visit" neither collect nor send personal information. A code holds only the kind, spot and direction of decor, painted ground and expansion stage; no animals, hearts, names or device info. The featured garden list reads a file from this site, where the game lives. There's no feature for players to exchange anything directly.
